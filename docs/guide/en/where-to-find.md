# Where to find everything

[Back to contents](README.md) · [Español](../es/where-to-find.md) · [Русский](../ru/where-to-find.md)

The first part of this page talks about the places in general, with no spoilers. The exact places, what each one holds and where every
item turns up come later, after a [spoiler warning](#spoilers-from-here-on).

## The places, in general

The mod's content is spread two ways:

- **Fixed places:** a few buildings on the map hold a set of items, the lore notes and the implants. Their content appears the first
  time a player gets close.
- **Random loot:** across the whole map, some items can turn up in containers of the right kind of building, the way vanilla loot does.

### The campuses

Two universities hold the **civilian side** of the story: lab prototypes, a student's notes and what you need to make your own
immunosuppressants. They are ordinary buildings with ordinary zombies, which makes them the natural **early-game** goal. Explore them
from top to bottom. By default both have content, and a sandbox option can leave only one.

**If you start with nothing, look for a university.** That is where the civilian side begins: it needs no skill to walk in, and the
first things you find there are what you need to get an implant running.

### The military base

A military research facility holds the **military side**: the strongest implants, the best medicine and most of the story. It is large
and goes deep underground. It's a **late-game** goal: bring light, supplies and a way back out.

### Ex-subjects

Some zombies still carry their implant. They are the **ex-subjects**: you recognize them by the **implant glowing on their back**, and
they move faster than a normal zombie. They roam near the fixed places, and if they despawn, others can show up later. Kill one and
extract its implant from the body (see [Extraction](surgery-and-crafting.md#extracting-an-implant-from-a-body)).

### Where random loot shows up

It's worth searching:

- **medical places:** hospitals, clinics, pharmacies and medical storage (drugs and samples);
- **labs and schools:** labs, classrooms, libraries and lockers (notebooks, notes, the guide books, now and then an implant);
- **offices:** desks and lockers (notes, parts, firmware chips);
- **electronics:** electronics stores and crates (implant parts, firmware chips);
- **army places:** storage, hangars and bunkers (military materials and firmware).

The strongest medicine and the military implants don't turn up as random loot: you have to go get them.

### Notes and dossiers

There are **20 notes in three stories**. Right-click a note → *Start a dossier with this page*, and file the rest as you find them
(*File in dossier* or *File all loose pages*). The dossier reads the whole story in order and marks the missing pages. **Complete
dossiers give lasting benefits**, and some of them are needed to build your own implants.

### A suggested path

1. **Early game:** a campus. Look for the implant, the notes and the recipes, and deal with the ex-subject. Learn to make
   immunosuppressants: you'll need them.
2. **Stabilize:** raise your adaptation to level 5 under coverage. From then on rejection stops rising by itself.
3. **Mid game:** level up Electrical, First Aid, Metalworking and Tailoring, and finish the student's story to build implants for your
   group.
4. **Late game:** the military base.

<details>
<summary><b>The campuses: names and contents</b></summary>

- **Louisville State University**, Louisville (around 12406, 2149).
- **Ekron Community College**, Ekron (around 765, 9841).

Each campus has:

- **The student's school bag** on the top floor, near the roof access. Inside: a **Prototype 05**, **2 homemade immunosuppressants**
  (3 doses each) and the **last page of the Student Lab Notes (5/5)**.
- **Student Lab Notes 1 to 4**, in labs and offices of the building.
- The **Lab Notebook (Synthesis)**, which teaches the immunosuppressant recipes.
- One **First Aid book, vol. 1** and one **Electrical book, vol. 1** in the labs.
- **3 or 4 Synthetic Compound Samples**, in storage rooms, labs and medical rooms.
- A **Prototype 04 (Defective)**, on the lowest floors.
- Half the time, **another Prototype 05** (a little worn, 1.2×) in some container.
- **One ex-subject** (sometimes two), in student clothes and with a lab implant.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>The military base: name and contents</b></summary>

**Military Research Facility** (around 5574, 12482).

- **Two Sealed Cryogenic Cases** (one on Realistic and Brutal), at the very bottom. Each holds a **Sandevistan XM-1** and a **Surgical
  Applicator Harness**.
- **Researcher Papers 1 to 11**, spread through the underground research wing (from level −13 down): the personal logs in offices, the
  official reports in labs. **Page 12** is at the very bottom, next to the cryogenic cases.
- **Depot Guard Notes 1 to 3**, on the upper floors.
- **5 clinical immunosuppressants** (3 doses each). They are **not** found anywhere else.
- **2 Black Lace Autoinjectors** and the **Black Lace Formulation Report**.
- **2 Military-Grade Microchips** (for the Mk. II).
- **A Firmware Terminal**, the **Cryo Chamber** chip (at the bottom, near the cases) and **2 more programmed chips** chosen at random.
- **Three military ex-subjects**, in army clothes and with a military implant.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Ex-subjects: details</b></summary>

- Lab ex-subjects are in the campuses (one always, a second one 60 % of the time) and military ones at the base (three).
- If they despawn, new ones can show up near those places: a 5 % chance each day, up to 3 extra per place (sandbox options).
- If a body disappears before you extract, the implant is left on the ground (worn 1.5×).

</details>

## Annotated maps

Every now and then you will find an **annotated map** in the loot. They come from the vanilla stash system and point at a place:
"neural study / volunteers wanted" over a university campus, or "restricted area" over the military base. Read it, look at the
marks, and use the vanilla button to copy them to your world map.

## A wandering ex-subject

Very rarely you may run into an ex-subject **far from the campuses and the base**: one that got away and kept walking. The spine plate
gives it away, and its implant is still in the corpse. There are at most a couple per world.
