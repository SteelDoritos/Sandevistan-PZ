## Quick start

### What the mod does

The Sandevistan is a spinal implant. Switch it on and the zombies around you move in slow motion while you run and strike faster.
Every second you pay for it: the implant heats up, your body rejects it, and your mind wears down. Push it too far and you end up
with cyberpsychosis.

### What you need

- Project Zomboid **Build 42** (42.20 or later).
- Optional: **Moodle Framework** shows the mod's states as moodles. Without it everything works the same and the states show in
  the floating panel.

### Getting your first implant

There is no store. You have five ways in (details and locations in [Where to find everything](where-to-find.md)):

| Way | What it takes |
|---|---|
| **Start with one** | The **Backstreet Installer** trait gives you a lab implant, an applicator harness, immunosuppressants, two suture needles and alcohol wipes to install it yourself. |
| **Start with it on** | The **Chromed** trait starts you with a lab implant already installed (1.1× wear, rejection 15), 24 hours of immunosuppressant and a 3-dose pack. |
| **Find one** | Lab prototypes are hidden in two university campuses and, very rarely, in labs and offices across the map. |
| **Take one from a body** | Some zombies wear an implant on their back (you can see it glowing). Kill them and extract it with a scalpel. |
| **The military base** | The best implants sit in sealed cryogenic cases deep inside a military research facility. |
| **Build one** | Late game: study an implant and craft your own replica from five parts. |

### Your first hour with the implant

1. **Install it.** You need First Aid, a suture needle (or needle and thread) and disinfectant, plus the applicator harness, or a mirror, a scalpel and
   painkillers if you operate on yourself without one. See [Surgery](surgery-and-crafting.md#installing-an-implant).
2. **Take an immunosuppressant right away.** Without one, your body destroys the implant in a few days (about four on Normal).
3. **Press Z** to switch it on and press it again to switch it off. Short bursts are the key: stop before the Heat bar fills.
4. **Check the Implant tab** in the character window (and the floating panel). It shows Heat, neural load, rejection, coverage and
   what each one is doing to you.
5. **Read the field guide** that comes with your first implant. It's eight pages and covers the basics.

### Three rules that keep you alive

- **Keep your immunosuppressant coverage up** until your adaptation is stable (level 5 lab, level 7 military). From then on the
  implant stops raising rejection by itself.
- **Watch your neural load.** It drops very slowly. Above 60 you get symptoms, and at 80 cyberpsychosis sets in.
- **Overdrive is a last resort.** It always ends in a collapse.
